\begin{abstract}
    We take a look at the game \emph{Blood on the Clocktower}.
    We demonstrate that the \st can always unambiguously answer the question
    \begin{quote}
        \itshape
        Is my ability the value of our canonical amnesiac mapping for player $K$?
    \end{quote}
    and that the answer is truth-functionally equivalent to the hot-or-cold question
    \emph{``Is player $K$ the Demon?''}.
    With this question we can therefore effectively turn every amnesiac into the strongest info role.
\end{abstract}
