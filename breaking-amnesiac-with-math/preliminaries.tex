
\section{Preliminaries}\label{sec:preliminaries}

We assume that you, dear reader, are familiar with \emph{Blood on the Clocktower} (\botc)
including the basic rules, factions and goals.
We will therefore only recall \botc basics that should be common knowledge, but might not be.


\begin{definition}[Storyteller]
    A \st is a member of the gaming session that acts as a neutral moderator,
    who's sole purpose it is, to make innocent players suffer in confusion.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Players' hidden agenda]
    A player's hidden agenda is to make the \st{ }suffer by playing
    as quirky, weird and time-consuming as possible.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Notations]
    For all following pages, if not otherwise specified:\\
    Let \playerset be set of players in a game \game of \botc
    and $\playerset_G$ be the cyclic graph of their seating.\\
    $\playercount=|\playerset|$.
    $\playercount=11$ means a game of eleven players plus storyteller.\\
    $P, K, D \in \playerset$ and are players, where $D$ is the \player
    that drew the demon token.
\end{definition}
